Behind the atlas

A little project
about big worlds.

Midnight Atlas is an independent creative website made to explore what an AI-assisted web project can look and feel like.

The idea

Curiosity is the destination.

This site is a fictional travel-inspiration concept: a calm corner of the internet filled with places, moods and short reflections. It is designed as a creative experiment rather than a real travel agency, booking service or authoritative guide.

The visual identity, page structure, written copy, HTML, CSS and JavaScript were generated with ChatGPT, an AI model developed by OpenAI. The project was created for learning and experimentation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

Some photography is loaded from Unsplash and typography from Google Fonts. Those third-party assets are not generated by ChatGPT.

The short version

Made with ChatGPT.

ChatGPT helped create the concept, write the content and build the website code. A human project owner chose to publish and may make changes to it.

Important note

AI can make mistakes. Destination details, seasons, descriptions and other information may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated. Verify all practical travel information with official and current sources before relying on it.
